From `casimir-field-equations.md`. Its summary of the division of labour is the part a spine author
would want: `P^2` supplies the dynamics, `W^2` supplies the constraint selecting the irreducible
representation, and only at spin 0 is there nothing to constrain, the single case where "the
Casimir eigenvalue equations *are* the field equations" is literally true. Three things block the
`W^2`-gives-Dirac route: order (`W^2` is second order, Dirac first), which Lorentz representation
(Weyl and Dirac share a `W^2` and are different equations), and the intertwiner (the Clifford
relation is a choice of map, and no eigenvalue equation supplies a map).
