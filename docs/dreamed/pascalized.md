# Pascalized: the dreamed tree, distilled

> **DREAMED, UNREVIEWED.** See [`README.md`](README.md). This page compresses the 58 essays to their
> claims (not their topics), each distilled in rounds until the next cut would remove the claim
> rather than its packaging, per `TODO.md id:a1aa` (Pascal, Letter XVI). It asserts nothing the
> essays did not; where a claim was later corrected or refuted, that is said in the same sentence.
> Figures, posters and reviews are out of scope.

## The tree in one paragraph

Three sessions (2026-09-01, 09-04, 09-07), 58 essays, one question put to the owner's own ideas: pushed
to a theorem, what survives? The answer is uniform and was never stated as the tree's thesis, so here
it is: **every seed that survived did so by shrinking to a smaller, sharper object than the one it
was named after.** The Bloch ball became a report triangle whose third coordinate is gauge (ratified
D1/D2); WiRoHSH became Whittaker 1903 plus a NO-GO; "encrypted algorithm" became three problems sorted
by who holds the key; the FHE-LLM wall became an invoice; the five-level laser became the second law
in rate-equation clothes; omniscience fell to arity, not physics; narrativium inverted its own myth.
What the tree established is therefore not physics but boundaries: eleven owner-authored lines were
located as wrong or unstated, and the cluster's working mode was self-refutation (three of four
speculative essays killed their own construction; four retractions across six FHE essays; the Z2 essay
refuted its parent; the audit found 5 of 37 citations wrong). The one place the tree produced a
measurement rather than a boundary is the scheduler prototype, and it found the ratified fork has less
content than the eight essays arguing it assumed.

## How the distillation was done, and where it stopped

Each claim was written from the essay, then re-cut three or four times from the previous cut, keeping
a round only when it was briefer **and** better, discarding it when it merely shortened. The test for
"better": a clause dropped because it hid a vagueness. Most claims fixed after two rounds. Six kept
improving through three or four, and those are shown as ladders below, because a claim that improves
through four cuts is one whose essay never found its point, and those are the carry-backs worth
applying.

### Six ladders

**lasercool, the crossover (`lasercool.md` §4).**
1. "The threshold at which stimulated emission starts to dominate and the threshold at which the exhaust stops carrying k_B-scale entropy per photon are the same threshold, n-bar of order 1."
2. "Stimulated emission dominates exactly when the exhaust stops carrying entropy."
3. "A laser cannot be the exhaust: the brightness that makes it a laser is what empties it of entropy."
Stopped at 3; a fourth cut ("the exhaust must be dim") drops the mechanism.

**layered core, the design rule (`logic-layered-core.md` §4.4, read with `logic-counterfactual-boundary.md` §6.4).**
1. "The Kernel's inability to express the Theory's provability predicate is not a limitation to be engineered around. It is the safety property."
2. "The core is safe because it cannot say what it supervises."
3. "A core that cannot name a proof is a dashboard, not a supervisor; its safety is bought by demotion."
Four rounds needed because round 2 exposed that "supervises" was the hidden vagueness: the sibling essay had already shown the core cannot supervise at all.

**zkML, the real wall (`zkml-proofs.md` §1).**
1. "A zkML proof over private weights certifies an existential, some admissible weights produce this output, not that the promised model ran, so it answers the seed only if zero-knowledge over the weights is dropped, which is the property the provider wanted."
2. "A proof over secret weights proves a model of the declared shape ran, not the promised one."
3. "You can prove you ran your model only by publishing it."
Stopped at 3; "secrecy and attestation are one property with opposite signs" is vaguer, not sharper.

**scheduler, the fork's content (`logic-scheduler-prototype.md` §2, §7).**
1. "The two readings are indistinguishable in outcome unless someone pays to produce the (0,1) report; the distinction is not free-standing information present in the data, it is a purchase."
2. "The fork's operational content is whether an independence proof is worth buying."
3. "Reading (ii) is reading (i) plus a purchase, and at any plausible price for forcing the purchase loses."
Stopped at 3; the price qualifier (break-even a third to three quarters of an ordinary proof) is load-bearing and survives.

**models versus knowledge, the decisive fact (`logic-models-vs-epistemic.md` §2.3).**
1. "Under (i), z = 0 forces r = 0, so 'I proved this is undecidable here, stop asking' and 'I have got nowhere yet, spend more budget' are the same point."
2. "A state over models cannot tell 'proved undecidable' from 'not yet tried'."
3. "A theory determines independence; only a search has ignorance."
Stopped at 3, which is the whole fork in nine words.

**WiRoHSH discontinuities, the dividing line (`wirohsh-discontinuities.md` §7.1).**
1. "Smoothness is not the criterion; real-analyticity is. A C-infinity bump with compact support is infinitely smooth and is not the restriction of any holomorphic function anywhere on the boundary of its support."
2. "The line is not smooth versus discontinuous but analytic versus not; a smooth bump is on the shock's side."
Stopped at 2. Round 3 ("a bump and a shock are the same thing to a Laurent series") is false: the essay measured that they differ in rate (sub-exponential against polynomial coefficient decay). The cut would have removed a measurement, so it was refused. Omniscience is the contrast case: its essay reached its own fixed point ("arity, not capacity; no bigger notebook helps") in one round.

## The compressed tree

### Bloch Truth, first wave (2026-09-07)

- **weltformel-impossibility.** The Faizal papers prove only that a theory interpreting arithmetic cannot certify its own truth or consistency (Tarski, which unlike Goedel I survives non-r.e. axioms); that limits self-reference, not physics. Two located errors: the Chaitin claim is false as written (counterexample "r = r") and "an inconsistent universe" is a category error. Cubitt-Perez-Garcia-Wolf is the sharper bound and kills only the oracle reading of a ToE. Both errors are absent from the published responses (audit).
- **logic-bloch-poles.** Three statuses cannot inject into two poles, so poles should be provable/not-provable; true/unprovable is refuted because Goedel's sentence is both. The ball is Kleene, not Belnap: nothing sits above a pure state, so no glut. Did not fully survive: the pigeonhole premise (every status at a pole) was retracted by admitting mixed states (models-vs-epistemic), and the report-format thesis was superseded by D2.
- **logic-bloch-gates.** z is a Lukasiewicz truth degree, r is settledness, and the angle is not a truth value: a circle carries no rotation-covariant order (torsion). CNOT trades radius for concurrence, r' = sqrt(1 - C^2); kickback is the b_x = -1 corner of that formula; the implication reading fails because H tensor H reverses control and target. Three wires for AND is a counting theorem. Contextuality is unavailable at d = 2.
- **logic-qutrit-su3.** The qutrit Bloch body is not a ball: det rho >= 0 gives |n3| + p_undec <= 1 (witness diag(-1/6, 7/12, 7/12) at 75 % of maximal length), and the antipode of a pure value is not a value, so no distinguished negation. "ZF without C" buys no completeness (AC's independence is an incompleteness). Deflated later: the inequality is p >= 0 on a simplex, not an SU(3) result.

### Bloch Truth, second wave

- **logic-models-ensemble.** A state over models is a measure on the Stone space of the Lindenbaum algebra, Cantor space for an incomplete r.e. theory; every such state is diagonal, so the reachable set is the segment |z| = r and the ball is empty; a simplex is forced because Boolean algebras commute (Bauer). Fatal, self-reported: coherent measures give every theorem probability 1, so (i) has independence but never ignorance. Later repairs: the Demski citation is Sawin-Demski, Vol is weaker prior art than claimed, and the uncomputability is stacked (Tennenbaum).
- **logic-epistemic-state.** Over four statuses (proved, refuted, independent, open), z = a - b and r = 1 - d put them at the poles, the circle and the origin, with |z| <= r derived from p >= 0. Found against itself: (0, 1) also holds "certainly decided, no idea which", Ellsberg's fair coin, so the report is not injective; and no quantum structure is needed. Corrected: its own identification of Josang's u with 1 - r is false (u = ind + open), so it under-claimed its novelty.
- **logic-models-vs-epistemic.** Build on (ii) in the report triangle {|z| <= r <= 1} and re-type (i) as the semantics the report must be sound against; the triangle is exactly the ball's (z, r) shadow. The claimed cross-essay convergence on encoding (b) is not real (both are encoding (a)), and the simplex convergence is one theorem applied four times. Ratified as D1/D2.
- **logic-bloch-phase.** Provability cannot be the phase: box distributes over the idempotent conjunction and an idempotent group element is the identity (the K hypothesis is unused, so no modal repair exists), and GL frames have no cycles. Proof-relevance ranks first but yields Z, which is blind to the roots of unity the gates write. Feferman 1960 confirms the gauge reading. Its recommendation "look for a Z2 grading" was refuted by its child.
- **logic-beyond-su3.** Density matrices of equal trace are never Loewner-comparable, so the Bloch body is an antichain: values, no connectives, and Kadison kills effects too. Ranked: Heyting plus Kripke, with GL beside as the decidable metalayer (Solovay-complete). Goedel 1932 ends every finite n-valued programme. Its "2-simplex in two dimensions instead of eight" was corrected: four vertices cost three reals, a Bloch vector's price; its Sperling-Walmsley caution was wrong (audit).
- **logic-simplex.** Four vertices are forced (three makes r identically 1 and is not closed under conjunction). The conflation is forced by mixing alone, since the ball's centre already is the even mixture of the poles. Connectives exist but indep AND indep = open, so no idempotence, no lattice, no Heyting algebra; the three-status fragment is K3 relabelled and the whole novelty is one table cell. Learning is an irreversible monoid, so the gate programme is lost and costs nothing.
- **logic-z2-grading.** Any group-valued invariant multiplicative over an idempotent operation is trivial, so Z2 collapses exactly as U(1) did; stronger, a Boolean ring has no odd part under any grading group. The survivor is the sign of the atom-occurrence permutation of proofs, composing under cut. Corrected by proof-gauge: the semantic determinant witness has period 4 in dimension, and the subformula-property clause did not follow; its BPI convergence was dissolved by logic-bpi.
- **logic-layered-core.** Layer 0 is a quantifier-free constraint language over reports, complete because too weak to describe itself; GL sits beside, not between. A core certificate is never a shortcut (Loeb, cross-layer); an exact reporter would decide theoremhood, so reports must be lower bounds; no truth-functional conjunction on reports exists. None of it is new: proof-carrying code, Milawa, Harrison's partial reflection schemas. Missed the report index as a fourth channel (counterfactual-boundary).
- **citation-audit.** 37 items: 28 confirmed, 5 wrong, 4 unresolved. Sperling and Walmsley (PRA 97, 062327, 2018, section IV.3) publish the geometry (true and false at the poles, an undecidable equator, a double cone); the logic is not published. That sentence became D2's thesis. The lesson: "abstract silent plus PDF unreadable" is evidence of nothing.

### Bloch Truth, speculative wave

- **logic-complementarity.** No pair of questions about a sentence can be complementary: both are functions of the sentence, and two functions of one point always have a joint (the meet), with every point mass sharp for both. "Name a pair" and "justify the ball" are one demand by published theorem (a state space is a simplex iff all measurements are compatible). Refutation needing no pair: proofs can be copied, and a non-simplex space is exactly one whose states cannot. Non-commuting updates leave a simplex a simplex.
- **logic-counterfactual-boundary.** Neither addition nor multiplication alone is fatal (Presburger, Skolem); the pair is, through pairing, which is quadratic. Addition plus the bare predicate "is a square" already defines multiplication: the cliff is reached by adding a set, not an operation, and the zone before it is an antichain (Cobham-Semenov). A core that cannot interpret arithmetic cannot represent a proof, so it is a dashboard, not a supervisor. Physics can move computability, never definability.
- **logic-proof-gauge.** A discrete connection is flat iff it has a potential, and confluent normalisation supplies one, so Cut Gauge is exactly flat, with trivial holonomy on every loop over a set base; the sibling's sign is a potential, not a holonomy (the braiding is a second vertex, not a loop). Where confluence fails (classical cut), all proofs collapse to one class (Lafont): constant, not curved. The forcing-monodromy conjecture dies to directedness (S4.2). Verdict: a reformulation, not a theory.
- **logic-thermodynamics.** A partition function over proofs exists iff beta > log a, with mean proof length diverging at T_c = 1/log a (Hagedorn); but a is a constant of the proof system, equal for one theorem and for all (2.3445 both), so the temperature has no goal. r is not an order parameter (sigmoid in budget, non-monotone in clause density); z is what sharpens at the SAT threshold. Landauer is 10^11 to 10^22 from binding. A costume over one theorem about geometric series.
- **logic-bpi.** BPI is real and strictly weaker than AC, and it is about the wrong object: a countable Boolean algebra has a prime ideal in ZF by enumeration (machine-checked), and every Lindenbaum algebra of a countable language is countable. The live axis is computability (no completion of PA is computable, by Rosser) and WKL_0, not choice; the ensemble essay's measure needs DC, which is incomparable with BPI. "ZF without C" is inert in both directions.
- **logic-scheduler-prototype.** Readings (i) and (ii) agree to every digit until an independence proof is bought; at cost 80 against a mean proof cost of 40, (ii) loses at every independent fraction; break-even is 12 to 29 units. The regime where the distinction pays and the regime where it is affordable do not overlap. The conflation at (0, 1) costs a third of throughput, and (i) is immune to it. The interface admits removal, never prioritisation: abandon-and-revive ties round-robin exactly. Stipulation: independence is labelled by the corpus, not found by a prover.

### FHE cluster (2026-09-04, plus fhe-counting from 09-01)

- **fhe-toy-enumeration.** A strict two-operation scheme on a k-bit word keeps log2 k key bits (the Frobenius maps) against the OTP's k; key entropy and functional completeness are exactly incompatible; randomising the encryption buys zero key ambiguity (mean 1.000 consistent keys). Whatever makes FHE hard, it is not the algebra. Confirms Boneh-Lipton for fields; the draft's "rings" was retracted.
- **fhe-encrypted-algorithm.** "Encrypted algorithm" is three problems sorted by who holds the key: private function evaluation (solved, O(k log k)), obfuscation (VBB impossible), circuit privacy (a cost). The 2^n program-bit floor is a lower bound over all encodings and is attained. An encrypted program cannot branch on its data, so it is a circuit; Euclid pays a factor near 2.5 and a general program has no worst case to pad to.
- **fhe-llm.** No theorem forbids an FHE-LLM; the gap is 10^3 to 10^4 in latency and 10^6 in bandwidth. Matmuls are not the problem (under 6 % of multiplications are ciphertext-times-ciphertext); nonlinearities are. The client should own the vocabulary boundary: it deletes the vocabulary softmax exactly and saves 31x bandwidth. Corrected after audit: no end-to-end pure-FHE generative datapoint exists, and "FHE gives zero integrity" was retracted by model-attestation.
- **model-attestation.** Model identity is a two-sample test, cheaper than correctness; an activation fingerprint carries about 150 times the evidence of a sampled token (2 tokens against 300). Under FHE a provider cannot recognise an audit, so encryption makes sampled integrity checking unevadable; at perfect recognition no audit rate works. Retracts its sibling's gap-gating as non-novel (DiFR).
- **zkml-proofs.** zkML overhead is 10^3.2 to 10^3.8 and sublinear in model size (7B to 13B: 1.86x parameters, 1.30x time), so it is engineering, and a proof-system change bought 185x in one year while MSM accelerators would buy 1.04x. You can prove you ran your model only by publishing it (Hollow-LLM). Commit-then-challenge makes sampled proving a 3.66x business at a 1000x bond; naive zk-over-FHE multiplies to 10^7.
- **trustless-distributed-ai.** Autoregressive decoding is predictive coding, so float drift compounds (measured: a 4096-token answer reproduces about 13 % of the time even at the low observed flip rate); codecs fixed this by mandating bit-exact integer transforms, and integer transformers exist. Convergence claim retracted in part: an integer model is bit-exactly verifiable, but its nonlinearities (bit-shifts, integer sqrt) are the hardest BFV primitives, so the confidentiality leg is open. "Trustless" trades one party's honesty for many parties' independence.
- **fhe-counting.** Three counting debts closed with frozen signatures (2^{m 2^n}, (2^n)!, binomial(2^n, 2^{n-1})). The fourth, balanced iff semi-destructive, is an identification: a proof fixes what the words mean and verifies nothing until the owner ratifies the definition (true at n = 2 by brute force once he does). The id:76e5 Stirling finding is confirmed: a non-shrinking +0.4068-bit offset.

### WiRoHSH (2026-09-01)

- **wirohsh-splats.** The 3D reduction closes because the Wick rotation makes the direction null (grad u dot grad u = 0), so any twice-differentiable profile is harmonic; that is Whittaker 1903, and the b family is redundant (x_phibar = -d/dphi x_phi). NO-GO on id:ff32: a splat is one-sided, there is no wave equation in the pipeline, the domain is wrong, the compression runs the wrong way. Corrected: "no annulus contains the real line" was too strong (entire profiles are global).
- **wirohsh-discontinuities.** Non-holomorphic data survives as a Sato hyperfunction boundary value once the common annulus is given up; regularity is read off the growth of the Laurent coefficients. The line is analytic versus not, and a smooth bump is on the shock's side, so wirohsh.md:82 is false as written. The delta is the boundary value of the m = -1 Laurent term.
- **wirohsh-refraction.** In 1+1 the conformal class survives any index profile (rays, Fermat, Snell), but harmonicity dies to a drift term; the gauge w = sqrt(n) f trades it for a potential V that vanishes iff n is constant or A/(x - x0)^2. Fresnel is the two-phase Schwarz reflection principle with r = +1 and -1 as its limits; the acoustic weight 1/rho produces the impedance automatically. The two-sided Wick rotation worry is a gauge artifact.
- **wirohsh-ladder.** The ladder's parity is not Huygens: d = 1 is the ladder's best floor and Huygens' exception, and descent (which flips parity) is what the ladder cannot do. The classical (1/r) d/dr ladder goes up, locally, parameter-free; the owner's goes down and hands back d - 2 continuous parameters per rung, the disease the rotation was meant to cure; the payoff belongs to the Fourier relabelling, not the descent.
- **wirohsh-approximation.** The missing cell in Fourier : transform :: Laurent : ? is the two-sided Laplace transform, spelled Mellin in multiplicative coordinates (the z-transform occupies the Laurent cell). A real-analytic function has no compact support (Lean), so the basis can never localise: a fifth NO-GO reason. A basis is geometric only when the data is analytic on its own domain; Fourier wins on periodic data; Gibbs is 8.95 % in both.

### Thermodynamics, entropy, laser wing (2026-09-01)

- **lasercool.** Entropy per photon is set by mode occupation, not coherence. An atom sheds at most k_B T_rec/T_D = 4 omega_rec/Gamma per scattered photon (2.5e-3 k_B for Rb87) against at least 2 k_B per fluorescence photon: margin about 10^3, not 10^7. A laser cannot be the exhaust, because the brightness that makes it a laser is what empties it of entropy. Reabsorption is a rate problem.
- **five-level-laser.** The exact gain numerator's only positive term requires Q < 0, so inversion and cooling are mutually exclusive at any pump strength and any laser intensity: Scovil-Schulz-DuBois generalised, the second law as algebra. The dictated "relax 2 to 3" plus net cooling is inconsistent; the step must run uphill. As a fluorescence cooler it works and is dominated by two-level anti-Stokes.
- **photon-energy-scaling.** No COP-optimal photon energy exists: COP is monotone and Carnot is its supremum at every frequency. A power optimum exists because A is proportional to nu^3, at hν - μ = [4 + W_0(-4 e^{-4})] k_B T = 3.92 k_B T, Wien's law with exponent 4 (12.2 µm at 300 K), nearly independent of operating point. - **wick-entropy.** The Laurent index and the Matsubara index are one basis under z = exp(2 pi (sigma + i tau)/(h-bar beta c)); reading the polar angle as the thermal circle forces beta = 2 pi r/(h-bar c), Unruh: the owner compactified the wrong circle, and the wrong one is the interesting one. - **lambertw-statistics.** The bosonic inversion needs the W_{-1} branch; the principal branch (every CAS default) returns beta E_1 = 0 identically. The fermionic inversion always exists (max beta E = W_0(1/e) exactly) and is two-valued. The N family is Gentile statistics, not anyons, and stops closing in W at N >= 3.
- **acoustics.** acoustics.md never derives the wave equation and states its adiabatic assumption only through the notation c^2/gamma := nRT/m.
