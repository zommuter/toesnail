# Two posters

These are two posters for a twelve-year-old. Each one has a big picture, a few short lines, and
one idea you should be able to get from across a room in about fifteen seconds. The grown-up
versions of exactly the same material, with the real maths and the sources, live in the `fig-`
pages next door, starting with [`fig-the-object`](./fig-the-object.md).

The careful bit is the difference between the two sentences in the first row. "This sentence is a
lie" just spins; it never lands on true or on false. Goedel's sentence lands: it comes out **true**,
and its own words tell you that no proof of it exists.
