footnote at l.79 tells the reader complex numbers "can't be ordered! There is no unique way to

## 3. Omniscience fails by arity, not by capacity

The spine's `#TODO: try and proof whether omniscience is impossible?` sits at l.44.
[`omniscience.md`](omniscience.md) answers it with no physics at all. A knower inside the thing it
knows is a map from its own states to descriptions whose domain includes its own states. That shape
cannot be point-surjective whenever the answer type carries a fixed-point-free operation such as
negation. It is Lawvere's fixed-point theorem, proved from scratch in `lean/Omniscience.lean`.

The essay's second contribution is precision about which theorem applies. `docs/se-corpus.md` row
**M-5** welds the liar paradox, describability-uncountability, Lawvere and the Goedel/Lean bridge
into one line; they are four theorems with four different hypotheses, and **Goedel I does not
apply** to the owner's state unless he commits to knowledge being a recursively axiomatized formal
theory, which is a large extra premise that would have to be stated rather than smuggled.

mass is a nontrivial cohomology class. In the Poincare algebra that same bracket is nonzero, so the
identical central term is removable by redefining the energy generator.

## 8. Two negative results, briefly

**The photon file.** [`photon-localizability.md`](photon-localizability.md) checked the owner's
Gaussian ansatz and found two things that have nothing to do with the theorem the file was flagged
for. The ansatz does not solve the wave equation, vanishing only on a measure-zero hypersurface, and
a per-index width makes the ansatz's *form* frame-dependent, because a boost mixes components and a
sum of unequal-width Gaussians is not a Gaussian; covariance collapses it to a constant four-vector
times one scalar. Both are filed in `REVIEW_ME.md` `id:8e64` and open. The essay is equally firm the
other way: the derivative line the file stops on is **correct**, and the Newton-Wigner caution flag
is aimed at a target the file does not present, because Newton-Wigner constrains an **operator** on
a one-particle Hilbert space while the object in the file is a classical **field**. The trigger for
the misreading is the word "photon" in the title, not the Gaussian.

**Why three plus one.** [`why-three-plus-one.md`](why-three-plus-one.md) closes Ehrenfest's argument
exactly: at any stationary radius of the effective potential of a central force obeying Gauss's law,
the second derivative is coupling-free and positive precisely when the spatial dimension is below
four, proved in Lean for general dimension. Then it declines to sell it as more than it is. Tegmark
disclaims deriving anything, in his own quoted words placing the burden of proof on the critic; the
arguments are stability or anthropic arguments that vary the dimension while holding the FORM of the
laws fixed, which the essay names as the strongest objection and has no answer to; and the repo's
own WiRoHSH ladder **adds nothing** to the question, because a solution technique that works in
every dimension cannot select one, and its best floor is the dimension every other argument calls a
dead world. The essay's value is in saying so.
